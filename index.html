001 / ANALOG DESIGNCONCEPT SKETCH · PROJECT CAPTURES TO COME
FEATURED PROJECT 2025

Common-Source
Amplifier

Designer & Simulator

Cadence VirtuosoMOSFETAnalog

I designed and simulated a common-source MOSFET amplifier in Cadence, characterizing DC bias, small-signal gain, frequency response, transient behavior, and THD. I compared resistor and current-source loads, then added source degeneration to explore the tradeoff between gain and linearity.

Open the project notes

The study follows the amplifier from its DC operating point to small-signal and transient behavior. The key comparison is how load choice and source degeneration change gain and distortion.

Schematic, simulation captures, and measured results have not been added yet.

From logic to lights.

I built a hexadecimal counter, edge detector, display sequencer, and adders in Verilog. Testbenches let me check clocks, resets, and timers before verifying the output and debugging waveforms on an Artix-7 board.

ECE 316 digital logic labs included ripple-carry and 4-bit carry-lookahead adders. Verification covered individual modules and their timing behavior, followed by hardware checks.
